Protein Design Laboratory / 단백질 설계 실험실

Browser inference: LocalFold (https://github.com/sokrypton/localfold).
The exact source revision and copied module list are in vendor/localfold/PROVENANCE.json.
LocalFold code is under the Beer-Ware license; see vendor/localfold/LICENSE.
Our model registry retains only the ESMFold2 Experimental Fast 300M pair.

Prediction model: Biohub/ESMFold2-Experimental-Fast-base300M-step1500k (MIT).
Language model: ESM-C 300M, with its matching ESMFold2 input shim.
This is a quantized experimental checkpoint, not ESMFold v1 or the full ESMFold2 6B model.
The original model card is model-card.md.
Confidence: the Synthyra/FastPLMs confidence head on the frozen experimental trunk.
The Synthyra/ESMFold2-300 model card declares MIT; see
vendor/localfold/confidence-model-card.md.
FastPLMs license: Apache-2.0; see vendor/localfold/LICENSE-confidence.
The model weights are pinned to immutable Hugging Face revisions in
vendor/localfold/src/bundles/manifests/index.js and are SHA-256 checked by the loader.
Weight files are downloaded by the browser and are not included in the repository.

Molecular viewer: 3Dmol.js 2.5.5 (BSD-3-Clause).
Source and license: /assets/home/vendor/3Dmol-2.5.5.min.js,
/assets/home/vendor/LICENSE-3Dmol.txt.

Chemical structure drawings: SmilesDrawer 2.1.7 (MIT), Reymond Research Group.
See vendor/smiles-drawer/LICENSE. Structures use neutral L-amino acid forms;
side-chain charge classifications refer approximately to pH 7.

Comparison: fixed residue correspondence, alpha-carbon coordinates,
Horn quaternion least-squares rigid superposition. RMSD uses all residue pairs.
TM-score: fixed correspondence, multi-start iteratively reweighted rigid fitting;
heuristic maximization with full chain length normalization. This uses a different
transform from the RMSD fit displayed in the viewer.
No claim of equivalence to TM-align or RCSB structural alignment is made.

Example sequences: villin HP35 (35 residues), ubiquitin (76 residues),
protein G B1 domain (56 residues). Examples provide sequence inputs, not a
prejudged conclusion about mutation effects.

ESM models and upstream components retain their respective notices:
https://github.com/Biohub/esm/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICE.md
https://github.com/Synthyra/FastPLMs

Amino-acid 3D structures: NCBI PubChem computed conformers, retrieved 2026-10-16.
Source CIDs, record URL and SHA-256: amino-structures-source.json.
https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest
Neutral free amino acids (L forms except achiral glycine); one conformer each.
