# Biology card illustrations, version 1

Ten illustrations approved by the site owner in the image-study review, generated with the built-in image generation tool. WebP exports preserve the full original composition; unit artwork is sized within 800 × 600 and activity artwork within 1200 × 675.

These are conceptual covers for units and activities, not screenshots, measured data, or validated molecular coordinates. The protein image is not a rendering of PDB 1UBQ. The mitochondrial traces are conceptual comparisons, not patient results.

Four unit covers: cell energy (mitochondrial cutaway), regulation and defense (neurovascular unit), gene expression (transcription), biotechnology (plasmid design).
Six activity covers: mitochondrial assays, protein variant comparison, BBB permeability, neural timing/inhibition, RNA folding, and X inactivation / chromatin.

Original PNGs and generation prompts are retained in the project workspace's outputs/biology-image-study-v1 review package. Existing older previews are kept for history but are no longer referenced by these cards.
